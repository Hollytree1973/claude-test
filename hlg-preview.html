Why choose HLG

Every animal treated as an individual

With a strong background in canine sports and sled-dog competition, I understand the unique demands placed on working and performance animals — as well as the importance of building trust with animals of all temperaments.

Whether it's a competition athlete, a recovering pet or a senior companion, I take pride in tailoring every session with compassion, patience and evidence-based care, using a combination of therapeutic massage, electrotherapies and individualised rehabilitation and conditioning plans.

Helping your animals feel and perform at their best.

Ultrasound Dog Teeth Cleaning

A gentle, completely silent and motionless Emmi-Pet treatment that removes plaque, tartar and bacteria to support better oral health — no anaesthetic required.

  • Up to 96 million ultrasonic vibrations / min
  • Reduces gingivitis & tartar build-up
  • Fresher breath & healthier gums
  • Emmi-Pet certified & CPD accredited
