# Parking Adjustment Reference — Gibsons Surveyors

Used in qualitative adjustment Category 11 (Parking) of the comparable matrix.

Apply where the subject property and comparable differ in parking provision. Positive adjustment = comparable has inferior parking to subject.

## Application Notes

1. **Garage** is treated separately under Category 12 — do not double-count. A garage with internal parking counts under Category 12 only.
2. **Off-street driveway (no garage)** — use 50–70% of the parking space values above.
3. **On-street permit bay** — negligible adjustment in areas with easy on-street parking; use 20–30% of values above in restricted CPZ areas.
4. **No parking at all** — full premium applies if subject has parking, comparable does not (or vice versa).
5. Always note the specific borough/area used and the adjustment applied in the comparables commentary.
6. [SURVEYOR TO CHECK] where the comparable is in a materially different parking zone to the subject.
